# Company portal flow — September 28, 2026

The company header now shows the existing Level-1 field-completion percentage and a direct jump to missing fields. This measures saved fields, not eligibility or evidence approval. Non-admin views disclose restricted-record limitations; the existing 500-record bound is retained.

A read-only next-action summary uses authorized company facts and existing information requests. It prioritizes an expired record, an overdue request, stale source checks, approaching renewals and human review before incomplete setup. Up to three distinct actions link to existing editors or requests. No facts, attestations, tasks or decisions are generated by the summary. Company-to-pursuit and notice links retain the selected organization and role-appropriate labels. Full renewal details remain in Radar rather than being duplicated on Overview.

Fragment navigation now focuses and scrolls to the revealed panel or record after rendering. It preserves browser history and mounted form drafts, supports record-to-record links, and falls back to Overview for an unavailable Decision Log. Phone navigation uses a horizontally scrollable section row.

The pursuit next-action order now recommends creating a response outline after a current signed-off bid decision and before submission handoff. Existing stale-evidence, sign-off and no-bid gates are retained.

No database migrations, permissions, provider configuration, customer-record mutations or changes to the fictional demo data are included. Unrelated local files, including presentation deliverables, are excluded from the release.

# Pursuit workflow

Company facts → official opportunity → cited requirements → reviewed evidence → assigned work → human bid intent → saved response → frozen review version → pricing/compliance review → final approval → submission authorization → human delivery → submission record and follow-up.

## Guide and next actions

The optional Getting Started card appears on authenticated Today. Its 12-step guide reads authorized saved records; visits and dismissal never mark a step complete. Dismissal is a browser preference scoped to organization and user. Workspace guide reopens it from secondary navigation. The primary destinations remain Today, Opportunities, Pursuits, Company and Assistant. Reports, account settings and help remain secondary.

The guide evaluates pursuit-specific evidence, tasks, decisions, drafts and releases only when that pursuit is loaded. Otherwise those steps say not evaluated. “Recorded” means the relevant record exists, not that the organization qualifies. Company Passport remains a review step rather than a blanket completed credential claim. Roles explain why an action requires another person. The guide never creates a duplicate pursuit, verifies a fact, approves a document or submits anything.

Next actions are deterministic: imminent/passed pursuit deadline, unresolved or stale requirement findings, assigned work due within a day, then unfinished authorized guide steps. At most one primary and three secondary actions are shown. Links preserve the organization and target the pursuit, requirement or task. The selected response uses the same pursuit actions and its existing missing-answer edit links. Workspace lists remain bounded, so guidance does not claim an exhaustive workload assessment.

## Human review

Verification establishes a sourced company fact. Evidence applicability and proposal-use approval establish its reviewed use for a particular requirement. A requirement finding records supported, blocked, clarification, review needed or a documented buyer waiver. These are distinct records. Only an executive can record a waiver with authority references. “Pursue bid” is a current human intent decision, not pricing approval or permission to submit.

Open the full notice and amendments. Candidate requirement extraction does not certify a complete register. Resolve stale findings, missing owners and writing gaps before final-file review. See [response workspace](response-workspace.md) and [approval/submission rules](approvals-and-submission.md).
